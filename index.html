Puzzle Warriorz

Three heroes, three puzzles, one battle. Slash with the Warrior, chain orbs with the Mage and fire volleys with the Archer, and switch heroes at the right moment to beat every slime.

  • 5 worlds and their bosses, from Slime Meadow to Slime Castle
  • Upgrade each hero's power, focus and special move
  • Plays offline, in 60 languages
  • Made for all ages, with accessibility options

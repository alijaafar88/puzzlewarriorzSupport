Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026 · App: Puzzle Warriorz (Android, package com.org.puzzlewarriorz)

Puzzle Warriorz is a puzzle adventure game made for everyone, including children. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. The short version: your progress, gold and settings stay on your device. The only information that leaves it goes to our ad provider (Google AdMob) and our crash reporter (Sentry), and, if you buy something, to Google Play, as described below. The Amazon Appstore version has no ads and no purchases, so only crash reports leave the device.

Information stored only on your device

  • Your birth year, asked once when you first open the app. We use it only to choose the right kind of ads and to decide when to ask a grown-up first (see Children). It never leaves your device.
  • Game data: levels cleared, stars, gold, upgrades, portrait frames and your settings.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this from the device. If Android backup is turned on, Android may also keep a copy in your own Google account's backup so it can be restored on a new phone; you control that in Android settings (Settings → System → Backup), and we can't see it.

Advertising (Google AdMob, Google Play version only)

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: rewarded videos you choose to watch (for double gold after a win) and, now and then, an ad between battles (never during one). To show and measure ads, Google may collect device information such as the device model, operating system, IP address, and, for players 13 and older, the advertising ID. How Google uses this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, we ask for your consent before any personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Children

If the birth year entered means the player may be under 13, the app tells Google AdMob to treat every ad request as directed to a child: no personalized ads, no advertising ID, and only G-rated (general audience) ads. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app has no chat, no accounts, no public profiles and no way to share personal information with other players.

Parents or guardians with questions can contact us at the address below.

Purchases (Google Play version only)

You can buy gold packs and an upgrade that removes the ads between battles. Every purchase is made through Google Play: Google handles the payment and your payment details, and we never see them. Google gives the app a purchase record (which product, when, and a purchase token) so it can deliver what you bought; the app keeps it on your device. If the birth year entered means the player may be under 13, the app asks a grown-up question before every real-money purchase, and Google Play's own parental controls can also require a parent's approval. See the Google Payments privacy notice.

If you reinstall the app or move to a new phone, "Restore purchases" in the Shop gets back Remove ads. Gold you had already received is stored on the device and isn't restored.

Crash reports (Sentry)

If the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a crash report to Sentry so we can fix the problem. A report contains technical information only: what went wrong in the code, the app version, the device model, the Android version and similar details. It does not include your name, your advertising ID, screenshots or anything on the screen. We have configured Sentry not to store IP addresses. See Sentry's privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information.
  • We don't ask for your name, email, contacts, location or photos.
  • We don't see or store your payment details; Google Play handles every payment.
  • The game works without an internet connection; nothing about how you play is sent to us.

Keeping and deleting data

Game data stays on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings. Crash reports are kept by Sentry for up to 90 days. To ask about or request deletion of any information, contact us below.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it on this page and change the effective date above.
